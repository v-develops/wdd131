OBSERVE THE NIGHT SKY

Stargazing is easier than you might think! You don't need expensive equipment or special experience to begin exploring the universe. With a little curiosity, a clear sky, and a few minutes outdoors, you can start discovering the Moon, stars, and planets visible from your own backyard!

Start exploring!

Ready to explore the night sky? Visit our Stargazing page to discover celestial objects, learn more about what you can see in the sky, and find inspiration for your next stargazing adventure. Click on Stargazing in the navigation menu and start exploring!

Why stargaze?

Getting started is very simple. Find a safe place with a clear view of the sky, away from bright lights if possible. Bring a comfortable chair, dress for the weather, and give your eyes some time to adjust to the darkness. A phone app or a simple star map can also help you identify what you see!

Start small and take your time! Choose one easy target, such as the Moon or a bright constellation, and try to find it in the sky. Check the weather and the time of sunset before heading outside. And remember to be patient! You are going to observe a natural phenomenon, you cant control the exact time it is going to happen, so just relax and enjo.
