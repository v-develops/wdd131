Stargazing Easy Starting

• This name quickly describes the purpose of the site: simple tips for starting stargazing.

Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to provide beginners with easy-to-follow tips for starting their stargazing journey and give some informations about astronomical events such as Meteor showers, Moon phases and visible planets in order to help people interested in watching these events to plan, and enjoy them.

Audience

My website intended audience is for everyone interested in this kind of information. Ranging from kids to adults.
